# Gemini Ultra-Realistic Studio Portrait Prompt

## Reference Style
Personal branding photography — elegant, confident, professional.

---

## English Prompt (recommended for best results)

```
Ultra-realistic studio portrait photograph of a woman, personal branding photography style.
She has long wavy balayage hair (dark roots fading to warm blonde ends), sitting in a modern
white boucle accent chair with warm wooden armrests. She wears a structured black blazer over
a subtle striped blouse, gold hoop earrings. Relaxed confident pose: body turned slightly
sideways, right forearm resting on the chair arm, left hand delicately raised near chin,
direct eye contact with the camera, soft natural expression.

Background: seamless neutral warm beige/greige studio backdrop, no distractions.
Lighting: soft diffused studio light from front-left, creating gentle shadows on the right
side of the face, subtle rim light, natural skin texture visible, warm skin tones.
Camera: 85mm portrait lens, f/2.0 aperture, shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the
face and eyes, slight bokeh on background. Shot from slightly below eye level.
Color grading: warm and neutral tones, slightly desaturated, editorial magazine quality.
Mood: elegant, confident, approachable, professional.
Photo quality: 8K resolution, hyperrealistic, photographic, no AI artifacts, grain-free,
sharp details, studio professional photography.
```

## Tips for best results in Gemini

- Use the **English prompt** for sharper, more consistent outputs.
- Add `--ar 4:5` or `--ar 3:4` for portrait orientation if using an API parameter.
- If results look too digital, add: `analog photography feel, film grain, shot on Phase One`.
- For stronger realism, prepend: `RAW photo, DSLR, professional studio photography,`.
- Iterate on lighting: swap `front-left` for `Rembrandt lighting` or `butterfly lighting`
  to change the mood while keeping the studio aesthetic.
